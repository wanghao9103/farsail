# Idle connection wake delivery

2026-11-12. Follow-up to WI-051: capture-lease renewal alone did not prevent this ColorOS firmware from removing both CPU locks after approximately five minutes on battery with the screen off. The physical unplugged sample lost heartbeats for 125 seconds, beyond the 60-second device lease.

Add one explicit, immutable, non-exported `ELAPSED_REALTIME_WAKEUP` delivery 25 seconds ahead while the authenticated connection foreground service is running, keep-online is enabled, its saved scope still matches, the phone is asleep and unplugged, and user-granted battery exemption is present. On Android 12+, scheduling also checks `canScheduleExactAlarms`; permission denial is caught. No new permission, shell whitelist, hidden flag, fake alarm-clock UI, audio or sensor activity is used. This phone's inspected `AlarmManagerService$3.set` automatically applies unrestricted idle delivery to user-power-whitelisted UIDs. This is evidence for testing this firmware, not a promise that all Android devices dispatch at that frequency.

Normal 15-second ticks retain the outstanding deadline rather than postponing it. An actual alarm consumes that deadline, requests a fresh heartbeat through the existing independent worker, and schedules the next delivery. The worker still validates native scope and authorization. A broadcast's asynchronous CPU delivery ends when the request completes or after an independent eight-second timeout. It cannot turn on the display, unlock the phone, restore recording consent or approve remote control. An alarm received without the live service cancels itself rather than recreating hosting. Charging, foreground display, opt-out, scope changes and service shutdown cancel the relevant work; late deliveries cannot revive a closed schedule.

Local checks passed 20 JVM tests, debug lint (zero errors, 52 warnings, including the expected exact-alarm user-exemption guidance) and instrumentation-test Kotlin compilation. Four new tests cover deadline starvation by normal ticks, cancellation, late delivery after close and scheduling denial. Build `0.1.17-idle1` with the original certificate and unchanged verified native libraries. Physical acceptance remains pending: observe real alarm delivery after the five-minute lock-removal point, then verify uninterrupted heartbeats and approved remote access during a sufficiently long physical unplugged test. Do not mark the offline issue solved from unit tests or a USB-connected simulation.
